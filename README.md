# Back Plan

A phone-friendly daily plan for rehabbing an L4–L5 disc herniation, based on Matt Carmona's (@bigmona_fit) documented rehab of three herniated discs.

- **Today:** the day's exercises with sets, cues, and demo videos, plus a 10-second pain check-in.
- **Plan:** six stages with a clear check for when to move on.
- **Log:** pain trend and whether leg pain is moving up (good) or spreading down (back off).
- **Rules:** red flags, the pain traffic light, and what to avoid.

Plain HTML/CSS/JS, no build step. Exercise content lives in `data.js`. Data is stored only in the phone's browser.

Not medical advice.

Exercise photos from [Free Exercise DB](https://github.com/yuhonas/free-exercise-db) (public domain). Demo videos are embedded from their YouTube creators.
